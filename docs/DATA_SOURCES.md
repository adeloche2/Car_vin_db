## Coverage and reuse rules
- Record the real geographic/regulatory scope of each dataset.
- Preserve raw records, source URLs and document hashes.
- Do not infer global WMI ownership from a regional list without corroboration.
- Do not infer model from WMI alone.
- Do not assume manufacturer documents are public or freely redistributable.
- Respect source terms, copyright and attribution.

VehiclesDB attribution: `Vehicle data by VehiclesDB` with a link to https://vehiclesdb.com. Confirm the release's current ATTRIBUTION.md before redistributing data.
